# Weekly Meal Plan — Week of Jul 25, 2026

> Emma allergens: Cashew Nut (hives), Pistachio Nut (hives), Egg (unknown). All meal bases are Emma-safe. Adults/Grayson add toppings.

- Avocado x3, Blueberries, Strawberries (2 lb), Bananas, Granny Smith apples

## 🍽️ Dinners (5 nights)

### Night 1 — Sheet Pan Chicken + Sweet Potato + Broccoli
**Emma:** Soft roasted chicken pieces + sweet potato cubes + broccoli florets (plain, no seasoning)
**Grayson:** Same, maybe a little salt
**Adults:** Season chicken with garlic powder, Italian herbs, olive oil. Side of roasted broccoli with lemon.
**Need from pantry:** Chicken breast or thighs (Costco stock), olive oil, garlic, Italian seasoning

### Night 2 — Beef Taco Bowls
**Emma:** Soft ground beef + mashed black beans + mashed avocado over white rice (no salt/spice)
**Grayson:** Same + shredded Mexican cheese
**Adults:** Full build — beef, rice, beans, avocado, Tillamook Mexican blend, salsa, sour cream, lime
**Need from pantry:** Rice, canned black beans, salsa, sour cream, limes

### Night 3 — Spaghetti + Meat Sauce
**Emma:** Soft pasta + plain ground beef sauce (mild, low-salt)
**Grayson:** Pasta + sauce + Parmesan
**Adults:** Full marinara + beef, Sarvecchio Parmesan, garlic bread optional
**Need from pantry:** Spaghetti, marinara jar, olive oil, garlic

### Night 4 — Chicken + Veggie Stir Fry Rice Bowl
**Emma:** Plain steamed chicken + steamed broccoli/zucchini + rice (NO soy sauce)
**Grayson:** Chicken + veg + rice + light drizzle of soy sauce
**Adults:** Full stir-fry — chicken, broccoli, zucchini, red pepper, onion, garlic, coconut aminos or soy sauce over rice
**Need from pantry:** Rice, chicken breast, coconut aminos or soy sauce, sesame oil optional

### Night 5 — Turkey Meatball Bowls
**Emma:** Plain ground turkey meatballs (breadcrumbs only — NO egg binder), soft pasta (no salt)
**Grayson:** Meatballs + pasta + mild marinara + Parmesan
**Adults:** Meatballs + pasta + marinara + Parmesan + crushed red pepper
**Need from pantry:** Breadcrumbs, pasta or rice, marinara

## 🌟 Bonus Meals / Flex Nights

### Burrata Caprese Night (light dinner or starter)
Fresh burrata with sliced tomatoes (grab tomatoes), drizzle olive oil, salt, basil. Serve with Brownberry whole wheat bread. Add prosciutto for adults if desired.

### Cauliflower Roast (side dish all week)
Jumbo cauliflower — cut into florets, roast with olive oil, garlic powder, salt. Great side for any night. Adults can add parmesan or buffalo sauce.

### Zucchini add-ons
Slice and sauté zucchini with onion and olive oil — easy add-on side for taco bowls or pasta nights.
