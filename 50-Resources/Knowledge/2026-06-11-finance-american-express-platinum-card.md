# American Express Platinum Card Credits Expiring June 30th

## Summary
This content highlights various American Express Platinum card credits totaling $525 that are expiring on June 30th. It encourages cardholders to utilize these credits, which include hotel, dining, retail, and other monthly benefits, before they expire to maximize their value.

## Key Points
- Amex Platinum cardholders have $525 in credits expiring on June 30th.
- Credits include $300 for hotels (Fine Hotels and Resorts Collection or Hotel Collection), $100 for Resy, $75 for Lululemon, and $50 for Saks.
- Hotel credits offer additional perks like property credits, room upgrades, and flexible check-in/out.
- Regular monthly credits (Uber, Walmart, Digital Entertainment) should also be maximized.
- Cardholders can find full details and enrollment options in their Amex dashboard.

## Notable
> Go maximize your credits!
