# LLM Council: An AI Board of Directors for Better Decision Making

## Summary
The LLM Council, open-sourced by Andrej Karpathy, allows multiple frontier AI models (ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok) to debate and critique each other's answers to a single question.
This process aims to overcome individual AI model biases and blind spots by synthesizing diverse perspectives into a comprehensive, 'battle-tested' final response.
It can be easily set up in minutes using tools like Cursor, even without coding knowledge, effectively providing a free AI 'board of directors' for strategic decisions and validating expensive choices.

## Key Points
- The LLM Council is an open-source tool by Andrej Karpathy that enables multiple AI models to debate and rank each other's responses to a single query.
- It works in four stages: initial independent opinions, anonymized review and ranking of responses, active critique of arguments, and a 'chairman synthesis' into one final answer.
- This system provides diverse viewpoints, similar to a human board of directors, helping to identify blind spots, biases, and logical flaws in individual AI outputs.
- It's valuable for business owners to make strategic decisions, validate expensive choices (e.g., consultants, campaigns), review contracts, and find solutions to problems.
- The setup is straightforward using AI-powered development environments like Cursor, requiring no coding as users can use natural language instructions.

## Notable
> You're essentially getting a second, third, fourth, and fifth expert opinion for just a few cents.
