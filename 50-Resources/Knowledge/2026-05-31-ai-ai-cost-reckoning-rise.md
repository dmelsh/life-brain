# AI Cost Reckoning: The Rise of Token Discipline with Opus 4.8

## Summary
The launch of Anthropic's Opus 4.8, while making Claude smarter, has intensified the focus on AI spending. Companies are facing 'AI sticker shock' due to careless token consumption, leading to a shift from 'tokenmaxxing' to 'token discipline' as a crucial skill for efficient AI utilization.

## Key Points
- Opus 4.8 makes Claude significantly smarter but also inherently more expensive if not managed carefully.
- Viral stories of massive AI cost overruns (e.g., $500M on Claude) highlight the urgent need for cost control.
- The era of 'tokenmaxxing' (spending tokens to appear AI-forward) is ending, replaced by 'token discipline' (using the right model, in the right amount, for the right job).
- New Opus 4.8 features like 'dynamic workflows' and 'effort control' can dramatically increase costs if not optimized, despite some price reductions on fast mode.
- Companies are becoming surgical about AI spend, switching to open-source models, purpose-built agents, and intelligent routers to manage costs and avoid vendor lock-in.

## Notable
> The reckoning isn’t a verdict that AI doesn’t work. It’s the high cost for using it thoughtlessly.
